About Blueprint to 3D

Blueprint to 3D is an innovative web application that uses AI to convert 2D house blueprints into navigable 3D models.

Simply upload your blueprint image, and our AI will analyze it to identify walls, doors, windows, and other architectural elements. In minutes, you'll have a 3D model that you can explore with a virtual walkthrough.

Features:
  • Support for common image formats (PNG, JPG, PDF)
  • AI-powered blueprint analysis
  • Accurate 3D model generation
  • Interactive virtual walkthrough
  • Responsive design for all devices
